Modifying Segments

How to modify segments in mikGen.

mikGen mirrors each library's internals, naming conventions, and constants, so paths simulate and export the way your library actually behaves. For tuning details, read your library's own documentation:

important

Swapping formats resets all segment constants to the new format's defaults. Segments the new format does not support are converted to their closest equivalent.

Units​

Distances are always in inches, angles in degrees, and times in milliseconds. The speed scale depends on the library:

FormatMax speed scale
mikLib0 to 12 volts
LemLib0 to 127
JAR-Template0 to 12 volts
EZ-Template0 to 127

Constant Rows​

To show all the constants that can be modified, either click on the segment's name, or click the dropdown arrow on the right

Constants are spread out in rows, and generally categorized as lateral, angular, translational, and exit conditions

You can then modify the constants that the segment uses by changing the number inputs

ButtonAction
DefaultDefaults the constants in the selected row. Changes the default for all segments of that kind`
ResetResets that row's constants to default
ApplyApplies the constants in that row to every segment of that kind

You can also click on each individual constant in the row to make the buttons apply to just that constant not the entire row.
