# Modifying Segments

How to modify segments in mikGen.

mikGen mirrors each library's internals, naming conventions, and constants, so paths simulate and
export the way your library actually behaves. For tuning details, read your library's own 
[documentation](./getting-started.mdx#selecting-a-format):

:::important
Swapping formats resets all segment constants to the new format's defaults. Segments the new
format does not support are converted to their closest equivalent.
:::

## Supported Segments by Format

Each supported segment is labeled with your library's naming convention:

| Segment | mikLib | LemLib | JAR-Template | EZ-Template |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| pointDrive | ✅ Drive to Point | ✅ Move to Point | ✅ Drive to Point | ✅ Odom Drive |
| poseDrive | ✅ Drive to Pose | ✅ Move to Pose | ✅ Drive to Pose | ✅ Odom Boomerang |
| distanceDrive | ✅ Drive to Distance | ❌ | ✅ Drive to Distance | ✅ Drive |
| strafeDrive | ✅ Strafe Distance (Holonomic only) | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| pointTurn | ✅ Turn to Point | ✅ Turn to Point | ✅ Turn to Point | ✅ Odom Turn |
| angleTurn | ✅ Turn to Angle | ✅ Turn to Heading | ✅ Turn to Angle | ✅ Turn |
| angleSwing | ✅ Swing to Angle | ✅ Swing to Angle | ✅ Swing to Angle | ✅ Swing |
| pointSwing | ✅ Swing to Point | ✅ Swing to Point | ❌ | ❌ |
| wait | ✅ Wait | ✅ Wait | ✅ Wait | ✅ Wait |

## Units

Distances are always in inches, angles in degrees, and times in milliseconds. The speed scale
depends on the library:

| Format | Max speed scale |
|----------|----------|
| mikLib | 0 to 12 volts |
| LemLib | 0 to 127 |
| JAR-Template | 0 to 12 volts |
| EZ-Template | 0 to 127 |

### Common Cycle Buttons

**Drive Directions**

| Button | Value | Behavior |
|:-:|-|-|
| <Fwd className="cycle-icon" /> | `forwards` | Approach the target front first |
| <Rev className="cycle-icon" /> | `reversed` | Back into the target |
| <FwdRev className="cycle-icon" /> | `fastest` | Approach with whichever end of the robot needs less turning 

**Turn Directions**

| Button | Value | Behavior |
|:-:|-|-|
| <Cw className="cycle-icon" /> | `cw` | Rotate clockwise, even when counterclockwise is closer |
| <Ccw className="cycle-icon" /> | `ccw` | Rotate counterclockwise, even when clockwise is closer |
| <CwCcw className="cycle-icon" /> | `fastest` | Rotate whichever way reaches the angle in under 180 degrees |

**Swing Directions**

| Button | Value | Behavior |
|:-:|-|-|
| <RightSwing className="cycle-icon" /> | `right` | Swing on the right side of the drivetrain |
| <LeftSwing className="cycle-icon" /> | `left` | Swing on the left side of the drivetrain |

**Turn Buttons**, on `pointTurn` and `pointSwing`:

| Button | Value | Behavior |
|:-:|-|-|
| <Fwd className="cycle-icon" /> | `0` | Face the target with the front of the robot |
| <Rev className="cycle-icon" /> | `180` | Face the target with the back of the robot |

| Button | Value | Behavior |
|:-:|-|-|
| <LockOpen className="cycle-icon" /> | `unlocked` | Turn will face to drive segment in front, or at 0, 0 |
| <LockClose className="cycle-icon" /> | `locked` | Turn segment will face point placed in [Segment Position Config](./segment-interfacing.mdx#segment-position-config) |


### Constant Rows

To show all the constants that can be modified, either click on the segment's name, or click the dropdown arrow on the right

<img src={require('./img/modifying-segments/dropdown-open.png').default} width="70%"  style={{ paddingBottom: '20px' }} />

Constants are spread out in rows, and generally categorized as `lateral`, `angular`, `translational`, and `exit conditions`

<img src={require('./img/modifying-segments/constants-open.png').default} width="70%"  style={{ paddingBottom: '20px' }} />

You can then modify the constants that the segment uses by changing the [number inputs](./segment-interfacing.mdx#number-inputs)

| Button | Action |
|-|-|
| Default | Defaults the constants in the selected row. Changes the default for all segments of that kind` |
| Reset | Resets that row's constants to default |
| Apply | Applies the constants in that row to every segment of that kind |

You can also click on each individual constant in the row to make the buttons apply to just that constant not the entire row.

<img src={require('./img/modifying-segments/constants-buttons.png').default} width="70%"  style={{ paddingBottom: '20px' }} />
