# Modifying Segments

mikGen mirrors each library's internals, naming conventions, and constants, so paths simulate and
export the way your library actually behaves. For tuning details, read your library's own 
[documentation](./getting-started.mdx#selecting-a-format):

:::important
Swapping formats resets all segment constants to the new format's defaults. Segments the new
format does not support are converted to their closest equivalent.
:::

## Supported Segments by Format

| Segment | mikLib | LemLib | JAR-Template | EZ-Template |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| pointDrive | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| poseDrive | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| distanceDrive | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| strafeDrive | Holonomic only | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| pointTurn | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| angleTurn | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| angleSwing | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| pointSwing | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| wait | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

Selecting the Holonomic option (available with mikLib) changes poseDrive to Holonomic to Pose and
adds Strafe Distance as its own segment.

## Segment Names

The same segment kind is labeled with your library's naming convention:

| Segment | mikLib | LemLib | JAR-Template | EZ-Template |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| pointDrive | Drive to Point | Move to Point | Drive to Point | Odom Drive |
| poseDrive | Drive to Pose | Move to Pose | Drive to Pose | Odom Boomerang |
| distanceDrive | Drive to Distance | - | Drive to Distance | Drive |
| pointTurn | Turn to Point | Turn to Point | Turn to Point | Odom Turn |
| angleTurn | Turn to Angle | Turn to Heading | Turn to Angle | Turn |
| angleSwing | Swing to Angle | Swing to Angle | Swing to Angle | Swing |
| pointSwing | Swing to Point | Swing to Point | - | - |

## Units

Distances are always in inches, angles in degrees, and times in milliseconds. The speed scale
depends on the library:

| Format | Max speed scale |
|----------|----------|
| mikLib | 0 to 12 volts |
| LemLib | 0 to 127 |
| JAR-Template | 0 to 12 volts |
| EZ-Template | 0 to 127 |

## Turn Targets

`pointTurn` and `pointSwing` don't turn to a heading, they face a coordinate. That coordinate shows up
in the X and Y inputs of the control config, where a drive segment would show its position:

| Input | On a turn segment |
|-|-|
| X, Y | The coordinate the turn is facing |
| θ | Greyed out and empty. A turn has no heading of its own |

By default the coordinate tracks the first drive segment in front of the turn, so moving that drive
segment re-aims the turn with it. Because it is derived, X and Y are read only while tracking. A turn
placed with no drive segment after it faces `0, 0`, the middle of the field.

### Locking

Every point turn segment carries a lock button:

| Lock | Behavior |
|-|-|
| Open | The turn tracks the drive segment in front of it. X and Y are read only |
| Closed | The coordinate is frozen where it was. X and Y become editable |

Locking freezes the coordinate the turn is currently facing, so editing the drive segment after it no
longer moves the turn. While a locked turn is selected, a dot on the field marks the coordinate it is
aimed at. Unlocking hands the turn back to the drive segment in front of it.

Whether the turn faces its target with the front or the back of the robot is separate, and stays on
the `angle_offset` cycle button.

To show all the constants that can be modified, either click on the segment's name, or click the dropdown arrow on the right

<img src={require('./img/modifying-segments/dropdown-open.png').default} width="50%"  style={{ paddingBottom: '20px' }} />

Constants are spread out in rows, and generally categorized as `lateral`, `angular`, `translational`, `exit conditions`

<img src={require('./img/modifying-segments/constants-open.png').default} width="50%"  style={{ paddingBottom: '20px' }} />

You can then modify the constants that the segment uses by changing the [number inputs](./keybinds.mdx#number-inputs)

| Button | Action |
|-|-|
| Default | Defaults the constants in the selected row. Changes the default for all segments of that kind` |
| Reset | Resets that row's constants to default |
| Apply | Applies the constants in that row to every segment of that kind |

You can also click on each individual constant in the row to make the buttons apply to just that constant not the entire row.
